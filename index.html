Pootle

Privacy policy

Last updated 2 October 2026

Pootle is a small app that gives you one prompt a day. It is built so that your information stays on your phone. In short: Pootle does not collect, store or share any personal data about you.

What Pootle keeps on your phone

To work, Pootle saves a few things on your device only: the prompt you were given each day, which ones you marked as done, your streak, the setup you chose (time, energy, solo or with people), any notes you write, any photos you attach, and your settings, such as the theme and reminder time.

All of this lives inside the app on your phone. The developer never receives it, cannot see it, and has no server to send it to.

No accounts, tracking or ads

There is no account or sign-in. Pootle contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting tools from third parties, and it does not build a profile of you.

The internet

This version of Pootle does not connect to the internet. All of its prompts are built into the app.

If a future version ever downloads new prompts, it would only fetch a public list of prompts and send nothing about you, and this policy would be updated first.

Photos, camera and notifications

Photos and camera: Pootle only asks for access if you choose to attach a photo to a day. You can pick a photo from your library or take a new one. A photo you add is copied into the app’s own storage on your phone and is never uploaded anywhere.

Notifications: if you switch on the daily reminder, Pootle asks for permission and then schedules a single notification on your phone at the time you choose. It is created on your device and does not use a push server, and nothing is sent to anyone. You can turn it off in Pootle’s settings or in your iPhone’s Settings.

Backups

If you have iCloud or computer backups switched on for your iPhone, iOS may include Pootle’s data in them. That is handled by Apple under your own account and is outside the developer’s control.

Apple

If you have chosen to share app analytics with developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give the developer anonymous, combined statistics, such as how often the app crashes. The developer cannot tell who you are from them.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything Pootle holds by using “Reset all local data” in Settings, or by deleting the app. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, that removes it completely.

Children

Pootle collects no personal information from anyone, including children. It has no accounts, ads or online features.

Changes to this policy

If Pootle changes in a way that affects your privacy, this policy will be updated and the date at the top will change.

Questions

If you have any questions about this policy, you can contact the developer through the support link on Pootle’s App Store page.
